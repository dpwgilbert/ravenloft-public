A young girl who, along with her brother, [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]], requested the party's help in rescuing their baby brother Walter from the house while their parent fought a monster in the basement.

Rose's body was found locked in her room in the attic, having died from starvation. Her ghost directed the party to the secret stairs to the basement and implored the party to continue their search for Walter.

After Rose's body was laid to rest in her crypt, she thanked the party and, along with her brother Thorn, faded from existence.
