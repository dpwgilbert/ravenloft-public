Mother of [[Rose Durst|Rose]] and [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]]. Wife of  [[Gustav Durst]].  Said by the children to be fighting monsters in the basement.

A letter from Mrs. Elizabeth Durst to a Mrs. Petrovna outlined how sacrificing an "innocent" to a planned ceremony may wield "better results".

Elizabeth Durst was turned into a black-hearted ghast by some occult ritual. While marvelling at her own "immortality", she goaded the party to see what she had done with "that bastard" [[Walter Durst|Walter]].

In a vision, the party learned that Dark Powers turned her into a ghast, granting her immortality (after a fashion), after she sacrificed [[Walter Durst|Walter]] in a cult ritual.
