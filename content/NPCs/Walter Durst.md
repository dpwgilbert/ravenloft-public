The infant brother of [[Rose Durst|Rose]] and [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]] was left alone in his crib while his siblings fled the house while their parents battled a monster in the basement.

A letter from [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] implies that Walter is a bastard.

Walter was sacrificed by [[Elizabeth Durst]] in a cult ritual to gain immortality.

Walter was used as the heart of a flesh monster that attacked the party until Short Notice was able to free Walter, destroying the flesh mound and putting Walter's tortured soul to rest.
