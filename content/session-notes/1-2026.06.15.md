# Session Overview

[[FMPc]] found themselves sheltering from a storm in the stables of the Nightmare’s Bridle tavern after their performance at Daggerford's Highharvesttide festival, along with [[Jerry Rig]], an eccentric gnome with a peculiar interest in the troupe, and a group of vagabond travellers, led by a man named [[Arrigal]].

Arrigal proclaimed to be a fan of the show, and suggested they visit [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]], the burgomaster of [[Vallaki]] for work. He stated that the baron throws festivals on a regular basis, and new talent is always welcomed.

The party enjoyed each other's company while the storm raged outside, sharing in wine and introductions. The group woke in the morning to Arrigal, along with their weapons and armour, missing and the stable surrounded by a dense fog. Navigating the mists, they found themselves leaving Daggerford behind and on an isolated road, upon which they were beset by a pair of wolves. Kexik called forth his lightning breath, much to the delight of Jerry, who delivered his own brand of lightning, and the wolves were dispatched, leaving the group to continue their journey through the mists.

Passing through a large set of gates, they found a body that contained a note from [[Kolyan Indirovich]], the burgomaster of the [[Village of Barovia]], meant to warn people away from Barovia, noting that his daughter [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] was recently bitten by a vampyr.

At a fork in the road, they encountered [[Specimen - 88]], who warned them of the dangers of Barovia (where the party now finds themselves), and offered his aid.

They came upon a small cul de sac of houses with two small children, [[Rose Durst|Rose]] and [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]], who pleaded for the group to rescue their little brother, [[Walter Durst|Walter]]. Armed with little more than their wits and magic, the group entered the house looking for Walter and equipping themselves along the way.

Moving quickly from floor to floor looking for Walter, the party found a dead adventurer, a letter from a [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] disparaging the family and denying a request for aid, and a secret libary full of books on the occult that Zavran deduced to be nonsense.

Making their way to the third floor, the party was attacked by an animated suit of armour which attempted to push Claire over the railing. Claire however, doesn't skip leg day, and had the strength to keep herself from plummeting down the stairwell. Rick attempted to insult the armour, but having neither sentience nor ears, the only emotional damaged inflicted was upon Rick himself. Unable to land a hit, the armour soon fell to the party's attacks, and as Jerry eyes up how he can scavenge the armour, our heroes find themselves ready to resume their search for Walter.
