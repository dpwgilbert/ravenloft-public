# Session Overview

As the party prepared to leave the Village of Barovia to escort [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] to [[Vallaki]], they found a page ripped from a book called _Van Richten's Guide to Vampires_ under their door.

> [!info]- Van Richten's Guide to Vampires (excerpt)
> In some shadowed corners of the world, the vampire reigns as a fearsome predator. Beyond mere bloodlust, these creatures are cursed with a range of abilities and weaknesses that make them as enigmatic as they are terrifying.
>
> Their bodies are resilient to mundane weapons, shrugging off blows that would fell most mortals and regenerating even grievous wounds in a matter of moments. They move with unnatural grace, their senses sharply attuned to the whispers of the night. But it is in their supernatural abilities that their true horror lies. They can bend the will of others to their own, ensnaring friend and foe with but a gaze and a whisper. They can shift form with the ease of thought, becoming bats, wolves, or even a sinister mist that creeps beneath doorways and through cracks. And those their fangs kill become vampire spawn—ravenous creatures with a vampire's hunger for blood.
>
> These creatures are not wholly invincible, however, possessing a tapestry of strengths woven with fatal weaknesses. Sunlight and running water can end their cursed existence, and wooden stakes through the heart will paralyze them as they sleep. They recoil from the sight of certain holy symbols, and cannot enter a residence without an invitation. They bear neither shadow nor reflection, and must return to their coffins, crypts, or graves to rest by day.
>
> It is said that the bloodlust of these creatures is an unquenchable fire that burns within their undead hearts. The young and newly turned are slaves to this craving, often losing themselves in a frenzy at the mere scent of blood. But those who have walked the night for centuries, as well as those with indomitable focus and will, may learn to temper this fire. Those who do so possess the rare ability to conceal their monstrous nature, retracting and exposing their fangs at will—a sign that the monster within is held at bay, if only while the vampire allows.
>
> To create a new vampire, a vampire must fully drain the blood of its victim without killing it—a torturous and careful process that can often take multiple nights. For some vampires, this process is a practical means of creating fresh and powerful thralls; for others, it presents a sadistic opportunity to slowly break their victim's will. Some vampires in the latter camp may even appear as an intermittent predator in the night, haunting their target over days or weeks before finally ending their suffering.

In the margins of the page was a handwritten note pointing them to "a small cache of items to help vanquish the evils of this land". The group showed this note to [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] and [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]], but they denied putting in their room, and neither could recognize the handwriting. When asked if they knew anything of this [[Rudolph Van Richten]] fellow, the siblings noted that he was famous for his writings on monsters and clashed with [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] many years ago, disappearing thereafter. How he came to Barovia, and whether or not he was able to escape its mists, is unknown.

During this conversation, the party saw a rat staring intently at them. [[Specimen - 88|88]] was able to grab the creature and [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] cast _Speak with Animals_ in an attempt to communicate with it. The rat noted that he served [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] and reported the party's goings-on to him. Ireena told [[Specimen - 88|88]] to kill the rat, which he obliged by crushing it in his hand. As the party packed their wagon to leave, [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] cast _Neutralize Aura_ to prevent her detection from fey, fiends, celestials, and the undead. Whether that would prevent Strahd's spies from reporting on her whereabouts remains to be seen.

As [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]] escorted the party out of the village, a woman - clearly distraught - emerged from her house and informed [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]] that her daughter [[Characters/Gertruda|Gertruda]] was missing and pleaded for a search party to be sent out for her. He assured her they would, and the party also indicated they would keep an eye out for her.

As the party makes their way to Tser Pool, they are stopped by a pair of scouts from the [[Village of Barovia]]. After ensuring that [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] was not travelling against their will, they noted that they were in search of the Lansten family - two parents and three young children - who went missing during the latest attack on the village, and asked for [[Players/index|Short Supply]] to keep an eye out for them. The party returned the favour, noting that [[Characters/Gertruda|Gertruda]] had also gone missing. Sadly, [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]]'s sketch did little to help identify her to the scouts, aside from providing her name.

Continuing on the road, [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] and [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] noted a spot along the road that looked like it matched the description of where the cache of helpful items from the note would be found, so the party made its way into the woods. Unfortunately what they found first was the Lansten family the scouts were looking for, recently turned into zombies. [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] displayed his ability to turn a profit anywhere by throwing good money after bad (guys) and doubling his investment, much to the delight of his compatriots. After dispatching the zombies, [[Kexik]] took the time to dig graves and inter the bodies while saying a prayer to Tyr for their souls. The remaining members searched through the woods for the cache. [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]]'s exposure to the outdoors was on full display, with him being unable to discern the (oak) trees from the forest, but [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] was successful in finding the cache under the roots of one of the large oak trees.

Within the cache they found the following:

- 20 silvered crossbow bolts
- a light crossbow
- two healer’s kits
- two vials of holy water
- one potion of healing

Returning to the road, the party continued on until they came to a crossroads, whereupon they met with another carriage, this one containing [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] himself. Strahd welcomed the group to his domain and claimed to be looking forward to how they conducted themselves during their time here. He introduced himself to each member of the party by name and indicated some interest he had of each of them. Despite Ireena hiding in the wagon, he called out to her, asking if she had properly introduced the party to his domain, and advised the party not to take stock in "folktales" that might have been shared about him. With introductions made, Strahd returned to his carriage and drove off in the direction of [[Castle Ravenloft]].

As the party continued along the crossroad to Tser Pool, they heard something large fall upon their wagon. Exiting, they found a large, owl-like construct, and swarms of smaller, raven-like constructs, blocking the road. Knowing [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] had gone in the opposite direction, [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] joined the battle, brandishing a silvered sword, to the surprise of a number of party members. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] decided to fight fire with fire, and summoned a bird construct of his own - fashioning an origami bird to aid in the battle. While the screams of the Greater Strix caused fear in some of the party members, they won out against the constructs without too much effort. With the battle over, the party found a raven who had been attacked by the Strix. After removing the barbs preventing the bird from flying, [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] cast _Speak With Animals_ to converse with the injured raven. While he was able to learn that the bird lived to the northwest, not much more information was forthcoming, and the bird flew away in that direction, leaving the party alone on the road once more.
