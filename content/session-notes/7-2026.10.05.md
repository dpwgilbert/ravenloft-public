# Session Overview

## Tser Pool Encampment

Having defeated the Strix, the party continued down the Old Svalich Road, arriving at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]]. The party is greeted warmly by the [[Groups/Vistani|Vistani]] who inhabit the camp, telling the party that [[Characters/Madam Eva|Madam Eva]], their elder, had foretold of their arrival. Over a shared meal of stew, one of the Vistani around the fire regaled the party with a story:

> “A mighty wizard came to this land over a year ago. I remember him like it was yesterday. He stood exactly where you’re standing. A very charismatic man, he was. He thought he could rally the people of Barovia against the devil Strahd. He stirred them with thoughts of revolt and bore them to the castle en masse.
>
> “When the vampire appeared, the wizard’s peasant army fled in terror. A few stood their ground and were never seen again.
>
> “The wizard and the vampire cast spells at each other. Their battle flew from the courtyards of Ravenloft to a precipice overlooking the falls. I saw the battle with my own eyes. Thunder shook the mountainside, and great rocks tumbled down upon the wizard, yet by his magic he survived. Lightning from the heavens struck the wizard, and again he stood his ground. But when the devil Strahd fell upon him, the wizard’s magic couldn’t save him. I saw him thrown a thousand feet to his death. I climbed down to the river to search for the wizard’s body, to see if, you know, he had anything of value, but the River Ivlis had already spirited him away.”

When pressed about the identity of the wizard, the Vistani said he name was "Morty, or something like that", and the wizard was bald. The party deduced that he might have been referring to the powerful wizard Mordenkainen.

While [[Kexik]] and [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] remained close to their wagon to guard a hiding [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], and [[Specimen - 88|88]] remained outside the crowded tent, the rest of the party entered to speak with [[Characters/Madam Eva|Madam Eva]]. She welcomed each of our adventurers in by name, and after inquiring as to where the rest of the party was, informed them of their fates: they each had a tie to Barovia, whether they were aware of it or not, and were brought together to overthrow [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] and free Barovia from the mists. Knowing that a party more predisposed to party tricks and revelry than overthrowing despots would need some help, she consulted her Tarokka deck to give the party clues to the advantages they would need to be successful.

### Tarokka Reading

#### Card 1

> **This card tells of history. Knowledge of the ancient will help you better understand your enemy.**

> [!tip] Three of Glyphs—Healer
> _Look to the west. Find water aglow with the light of the white sun and look deep within yourselves and without._

#### Card 2

> **This card tells of a powerful force for good and protection, a holy symbol of great hope.**

> [!tip] One of Swords—Avenger
> _I see the house of a dragon, and a heart - once pure - corrupted by hatred. Bring light to the house and peace to the heart, and you will have your protection from the devil on high._

#### Card 3

> **This is a card of power and strength. It tells of a weapon of vengeance: a sword of sunlight.**

> [!tip] Eight of Swords—Dictator
> _I see the shrines of murdered gods. Help the land find its peace, and it will reward you with the power you seek. Start in an amber palace of darkness._

#### Card 4

> **This card sheds light on one who will help you greatly in the battle against darkness.**

> [!tip] Artifact (Joker 1)
> _Look for an entertaining man with a monkey. This man is more than he seems._

#### Card 5

> **Your enemy is a creature of darkness, whose powers are beyond mortality. This card will lead you to him!**

> [!tip] Ghost (King of Hearts)
> _Look to the father's tomb._

After receiving their reading, [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] picked up the fourth card and began flipping it back and forth. Much to the surprise of the party, and [[Characters/Madam Eva|Madam Eva]], the card changed from the **Artifact** to the **Raven**. Eva nodded and indicated that ravens were good omens, and would also aid them in their endeavours. Before [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] could cause any further manipulation of the fates, Eva collected all of her Tarokka deck and wished the party good luck.

[[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] spoke with Arahja, the [[Groups/Vistani|Vistani]] with whom the group conversed with the most, looking for help identifying the locations associated with their reading. Arahja noted that there was a Shrine of the White Sun that could be found in [[Krezk]], and that the most likely place to find a man with a monkey would be [[Vallaki]], a town that held frequent festivals and would host the types of people that would have such a strange animal as a pet.

As the party settled down for the night, [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] and [[Specimen - 88|88]] found themselves craving more Dream Pie. Having already eaten the last of their own pies, [[Specimen - 88|88]] asked [[Kexik]] for his uneaten pie, which he surrendered without issue, and [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] went the indirect route, stealing [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]]'s pie from his pack. Both once again slept deeply and had a night full of wonderful dreams.

## Tser Falls

In the morning, the party decided to spend the day investigating [[Tser Falls]] to see if they would be able to find anything related to the wizard mentioned in the previous night's story. Both [[Fabulous Rick Landry| Rick]] and [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] were experienced enough survivalists to see a scrap of paper trapped between a couple of rocks which turned out to be a page from a spellbook containing the _Detect Magic_ spell. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] was able to recognize that he had seen the handwriting before, but could not recall to whom it belonged. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] then summoned a familar to search up the cliffside, and it was able to find a small scrap of black cloth, likely from a piece of clothing, hanging from a root growing out of the wall of the chasm.

Finding nothing else, the party made their way back to the safety of the [[Tser Pool Encampment]] prior to nightfall.  [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] and [[Specimen - 88|88]] once again found themselves craving more Dream Pie and finished off the remainder of the pies they had acquired the night before. After another good night's rest, the party bid their farewell to the [[Groups/Vistani|Vistani]] and made their way towards [[Vallaki]] once again.

## The Old Mill

The party spent the day travelling without much excitement, but as the evening approached, they spotted an old windmill not far off the road. Looking for a place to safely bed down for the night, [[Players/index|Short Supply]] were greeted by a raven, perched atop the entrance to the mill. [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] cast _Speak With Animals_ to converse with the raven, who warned them away from the mill, stating that "nothing good was inside".  As the party discussed what to do, they saw a cart approach the mill from the road, being drawn by [[Granny]]. [[Specimen - 88|88]], having eaten all of his dream pie, asked to buy five more pies, which [[Granny]] was happy to do at the cost of 1GP each. As she went inside the windmill to fetch the pies, [[Kexik]] heard noises from within Granny's cart. Lifting the tarp covering the wagon, he found a crate within which was a scared young boy. [[Granny]], who had just come out of the windmill with the five new dream pies, instructed the party to "leave her 'ingredients' alone" and asked if they would like to come inside to see how the pies were made.

The party, realizing the implication of that statement, prepared for battle. [[Specimen - 88|88]], enraged upon seeing someone caged, ripped the gate right off the crate, throwing it at [[Granny]].  All attempts at subterfuge were dropped, and [[Granny]] transformed back into a Night Hag, while beaconing her daughters to join the fray. The party traded blows with the night hag, with both [[Kexik]] and [[Specimen - 88|88]] taking a large amount of damage from the hag's claw attack, before they were able to dispatch [[Granny]]. No sooner had they done so when one of the daughters, another night hag, joined the fight, wailing over the murder of her mother. As the battle continued, the party heard another set of footsteps come down the stairs, prepared to join the fight...
