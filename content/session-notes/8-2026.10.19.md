# Session Overview

## The Old Mill Battle, continued

As the battle at the [[Old Bonegrinder|old mill]] continued, the party gave no quarter to the Night Hag sisters just trying to defend their baking operations from the aggressive gang of invaders that just murdered their mother. A rat scurrying across the threshold carrying a small polyhedral stone appeared to be a good omen, with most of the party, including [[Greavard]], and most especially [[Kexik]], in rare form, connecting on their hits more consistently than had been seen in the past.

While attacks from the hags could be devastating when they connected, [[Specimen - 88|88]] was able to reduce some of that damage with swipes of his tail, and the party worked together to ensure that the hags were always scrambling to connect - whether through [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]]'s _Bardic Inspiration_, [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]]'s _Visions of Annihilation_, [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]]'s _Silvery Barbs_, or [[Kexik]]'s ability to _Sap_ his target. While [[Kexik]] did come close to death, the party was unrelenting, with  [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]]'s origami creations flitting about the battlefield and critical attacks coming from [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], [[Greavard]], and [[Kexik]].

As the battle continued, the second hag tried summoning a Dretch and retreating up the windmill to distance herself from her attackers, but it's attempts to poison the party were completely unsuccessful, with only [[Greavard]] - undead and consequently immune - unable to avoid the attack. The Gretch did not last long after the failed attempt and, with despite the party's ability to cast powerful spells waning, they were able to dispatch the hags and secure the mill.

## A Night's Rest (?)

While [[Kexik]] took the hags, the gretch, and all the dream pies he could find out behind the mill to burn them, the party investigated the mill, finding a trio of strange elixirs, some cheap jewelry and, most importantly, two more small children: a seven year old boy named Freek and Myrtle, a girl barely five years old. [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] added fireworks to the bonfire [[Kexik]] had created, bringing some fleeting joy to the rescued children (including Ludo, the boy [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] was watching in the wagon). When the children were asked where they had come from, the children all responded that they were from the [[Village of Barovia]]. While [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] did not recognize the children, they recognized her as the burgomaster's daughter. When the party indicated they would return the children to the village, and their parents, the children were adamantly against the idea, saying their parents had willingly sold them to [[Granny]] for more dream pies. [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] indicated that [[Vallaki]] had an orphanage that they could bring the children to.

While the party settled in for the night, [[Specimen - 88|88]] and [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], having no more dream pie to ensure a good night's sleep, were plagued by nightmares and unable to get any rest that night - finding themselves exhausted as day broke. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] spent some time identifying the elixirs the party found, confirming that it was a wise decision not to partake of any of them, and noting that a couple of them would fetch a high price if sold.

## Approaching Vallaki

With the day ahead of them, and three additional wards to protect, the party once again made their way to Vallaki during the daylight hours. On the road, [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]]'s keen eye was able to notice that they appeared to be followed down the road by the same raven that had warned them away from the mill.

While most of their travel was uneventful, that peace was broken by a revenant who had wandered into the road to attack the party. While a few members of the party were frightened by the pallid skin and glowing red eyes of the revenant, and despite the exhaustion felt by [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] and [[Specimen - 88|88]], the party's ability to consistently attack with radiant damage, and [[Specimen - 88|88]]'s mysterious ability to resist the necrotic damage of the revenant's attacks, ensured the revenant was given no quarter. With a deft flick of the wrist, [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] sent a coin to snuff the light out of one of those glowing red eyes, finishing off the creature.

More eager than ever to reach [[Vallaki]], the party continued on down the road, eventually reaching the town proper, which they found to be guarded and surrounded by high walls, with a series of wolves heads impaled on pikes approaching the gates. The party presented their letter of introduction intended for the burgomaster to the guards, who accepted this as justification for their visit. The guards then demanded that the party register themselves and their weapons. While most were forthcoming about this, including [[Specimen - 88|88]] helpfully declaring himself as a weapon, [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] omitted a wand that he had crafted, stealthily passing it to [[Greavard]] to hang on to. After registering the party, the guards informed them of the laws applicable to all citizens and visitors:

### Vallaki Law

- Theft, murder, assault, and inciting public unrest are severe crimes in Vallaki.
- Once inside, the mention of [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]]'s name is strictly illegal, even in written form. Instead, if someone must refer to Strahd, they should call him, "the Devil" or something similar.
- Vallaki holds a festival each week in honor of the residents' general merriment and continued support of the burgomaster, [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]]. As guests of Vallaki, the party's attendance and participation in each and every festival is mandatory for the length of their stay.

After confirming acknowledgement of the laws of the land, the guards opened the gates and bid them enter:

> **"Welcome to Vallaki. All is well."**
