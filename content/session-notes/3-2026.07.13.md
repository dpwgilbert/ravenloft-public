# Session Overview

After finding a good home for the items found in the footlockers of the quarters found in the basement of the Durst House, our party set out once again to find [[Walter Durst|Walter]]. [[Kexik]] led the way, but scanning the ceiling for spiders, failed to notice the rotting boards of a pit trap and fell, taking mortal wounds on the spikes below. Not one to go anywhere without his tools, [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] grabbed some rope from his pack and scrambled down to Kexik and bring him back from the brink of death. Jerry's diminutive stature precluded him from pulling Kexik from the pit by himself, but the rest of the party banded together to haul them both out.

From there, and with a newfound abundance of caution, the team cautiously made their way further into the basement, coming to a small dining hall scattered with humanoid bones. The hall was otherwise mostly empty, aside from the Grick that Kexik found hiding in the larder. Built more heartily than most, the grick evaded a number of attacks before eventually falling to a True Strike from Jerry.

Moving towards the sounds of the baby's cries, and despite the party's best attempts to move cautiously, the party was beset upon by a quartet of ghouls, rising from the ground. While [[Specimen - 88]] nearly cleaved one in two with a mighty swing of his greataxe, he was not able to deflect all of the ghoul's attacks, but found that the necrotic effects of the bite not as dangerous as he expected. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]], not used to getting his hands dirty, was unsure about striking out at a ghoul trying to crawl past him, but took advantage of the opportunity and, using a knife found in the kitchen of the house, dug a deep furrow in the back of the ghoul . [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] drew on her divine power to turn the undead away (for a time), but Jerry didn't get the memo and shocked one of the retreating ghouls, turning its attention back on the party.

Dispatching the jolted ghoul, the party turned their attention to a painted wooden statue carved in the likeness of a gaunt, pale-faced man wearing a voluminous black cloak, his pale left hand resting on the head of a wolf that stands next to him. In his right hand, he held a smoky-gray crystal orb. Jerry used Mending to repair some ragged clothing found on one of the skeletons chained to the wall of this room and carefully wrapped up the orb. Zavran noted that it could be used as an arcane focus, and the party packed it away with the rest of their "liberated" equipment. Further investigation of the room led them to a concealed passageway that led to a ladder. Kexik cautiously poked his head through the trapdoor and found himself looking at the hunter's den where they found their crossbows.

The turned ghouls, no longer under the effects of Claire's spell, returned for blood, but found only eternal peace at the hands of the party.

Proceeding towards a door on the other end of the room, Jerry notes something odd about the door, but Claire, ever curious, touches the door, which reveals itself to be a mimic. The party, while weary from the preceding battles, steeled themselves and hammered away at the mimic, with Zavran's spellfire, [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]]'s dagger, and Kexik's longsword causing serious harm. Specimen - 88, while an imposing figure, found himself paralyzed by the narrow crowded passageway leading to the mimic and stayed behind to let the rest of the party deal with the mimic. While Rick was distracted, Kexik was able to finish off the mimic, stealing the glory of the killing blow away from the bard.

The party then makes a grim discovery. In a nearby makeshift bedroom, they find the body of [[Gustav Durst]], who has hung himself, leaving a note for his children noting the monster their mother had become, not realizing they were locked in their rooms above, awaiting for his return. From under the bed, the undead body of [[Elizabeth Durst]] rises, black heart visible from her chest, taunting the party for asking about Walter, the bastard child of her husband and their nursemaid. If they wanted to see what became of him, she encouraged them to look further in the basement. The party had hushed discussions on how to deal with the ghast, but since Kexik had convieniently forgotten her children's corpses elsewhere in the basement, the ghast did not seem physically aggressive. They settled instead for ransacking a stocked footlocker at the foot of the bed, ignoring the fact that the owners were in turn swinging from the ceiling and watching with undead indifference.

Reminded of the bodies of the children, and no longer hearing a baby's cries, the party made their way back to the quarters where Rose and Thorn's bodies were left, and the search for a resting place led them to the family crypts. Upon placing Rose and Thorn's bodies in their respective crypts, their ghosts appeared once again to thank the adventurers for granting them eternal rest as they faded with smiles and a look of peace upon their faces - a sight that gave Inspiration to the whole party to continue their search.

Looking through the remainder of the crypts, the party found a swarm of centipedes within the coffin of the corrupted Elizabeth Durst. Kexik let forth his lightning breath (again to Jerry's delight), and slammed the door of the crypt shut. Not so easily dissuaded, the centipedes crawled under and around the cracks in the door to continue their assault on the party. Zavran and Rick took turns insulting the creatures which, surprisingly, seemed to actually inflict emotional damage, and the centipedes were eventually crushed.

Resuming their search for Walter, the party proceeded further into the basement, coming to a closed portcullis. Weary from their many battles, the party decided to take a short rest before taking on any further challenges.
