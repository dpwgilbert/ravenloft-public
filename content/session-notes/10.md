# Session Overview

As evening approached, [[Players/index|Short Supply]] entered the open doors of [[St. Andral's Church]] to find a sermon underway. The party took a seat and waited for the sermon to end before approaching [[Characters/Father Lucian Petrovich|Father Lucian Petrovich]] and requesting sanctuary for [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]].

After having the altar boy, Yeska, clear the church of parishioners, [[Characters/Father Lucian Petrovich|Father Lucian]] told the history of St. Andral and how the church came to be protected from Strahd before noting that the bones consecrating the church had been recently stolen. Revealing St. Andral's crypt hidden below the dais, a few members entered to investigate, with [[Specimen - 88|88]] remaining above, too claustrophobic to enter the small crypt. [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]]'s keen eye noticed some dirt scattered on the floor, though there was nothing remarkable about it that would reveal its origin. Asked if he suspected anyone, [[Characters/Father Lucian Petrovich|Father Lucian]] admitted that he didn't trust [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]], who was employed as a gravedigger for the church, because he did not seem to have faith in the Morninglord. The priest kept him employed because his wages went to help the upkeep of [[St. Andral's Orphanage]], where [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]] was raised. With this lead, the party headed back to the orphanage to speak with him.

Still in bed recovering from his ordeal, [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]] folded under [[Kexik]]'s intimidation and let them know that he was offered 50 GP to find information on the church's relics from a bearded man in a hooded cloak. When pressed further, he noted that the man was wearing a pair of well made boots. After sharing information on the bones that he learned from Yeska to the man, he was offered an additional 250 GP to steal the bones and leave them in the Vallakovich family crypt - the biggest crypt in St. Andral's graveyard. With this information in hand, the party returned to the church, but not before [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] left his origami familiar behind disguised as a sheet of paper.

Working their way though the large crypts in the graveyard surrounding [[St. Andral's Church]], the party found the Vallakovich crypt without much effort. [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] made short work of the lock , noting that, given how easily it was to lockpick, it must have been opened recently. Seeing that no amount of _prestidigitation_ was going to clean the crypt, this time it was [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]]'s turn to wait outside to assuage his phobia. Passing alcoves of skeletons, the party entered a large room at the back of the crypt - finding a sarcophagus which had the dust upon it recently disturbed. [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] cast _Clue_ and was able to discern two distinct sets of finger and shoe prints, one set of which appeared to come from a pair of pointed-toe boots. Investigating further, [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] found a single long iron nail with a wide head on the floor, devoid of dust. Finding no other clues, the party made their way out of the crypt, with [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] noting that one set of footprints went in the direction of the church, and the other went towards the entrance to the graveyard. Unfortunately the spell's range made it impossible to determine the directions any further.

[[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] decided to cast _detect magic_ prior to leaving the crypt and during the long casting of that spell, a defensive spell within the crypt triggered, reanimating a number of skeletons which had been interred within the crypt's alcoves. A battle ensued, but [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]]'s casting of _Ominous Winds_ ensured that the skeletons faced an uphill battle in getting any of their attacks to connect. [[Greavard]] had no such issue, and was the most consistent attacker of the battle. Perhaps a dog fighting piles of bones gave him a natural advantage?

After dispatching the skeletons, the party realized night was upon them and shelter was needed. [[Kexik]] and [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] decided to stay in the church to protect [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], while [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]], [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], [[Specimen - 88|88]] and [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] crossed the road to take the headmistress up on her offer of a place to rest. Gathering together in the main room to bed down for the night, the orphan children turned the visit into a giant slumber party, with the children - young and old - joining the group in the main room, resting easy now that the demonic nightmare was over for them.
