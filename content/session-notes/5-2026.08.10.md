# Session Overview

After a night's rest - a night that [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]], [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], and [[Specimen - 88|88]] found to be full of wonderful and vivid dreams - [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] introduced the party to [[Greavard]], the deceased pet that he was able resurrect with the aid of  [[Kexik]]'s lightning. [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] explained that it was the pursuit of this electricity that led to him joining the party on that fateful night in Daggerford.

After [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] snacked on some more of [[Granny]]'s Dream Pie™️ as a quick breakfast, the party stopped at the local mercantile for supplies, selling some of the treasure they found in the ~~Death~~ Durst House . Finding the prices to be exorbitant, the only purchases made were by [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]], who needed supplies to copy some spells to his spellbook.

Leaving the mercantile, the party made their way to the Burgomaster's Mansion, though calling it a mansion was a generous assessment. [[Players/index|Short Supply]] found themselves at a house in a state of disrepair that looked as though it had been under attack for quite some time, with [[Specimen - 88|88]] taking note of both human footprints and wolf paw prints trampling the ground around the perimeter of the property. [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] took the opportunity to fix up the gate (as best as he was able) to prevent it from being a nuisance. At the mansion, the party was denied entry by [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] until [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] mentioned they had been sent by [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]]. Hearing this, Ireena called her brother, who welcomed them into the house.

Entering the home, the party found that the burgomaster, [[DM/Characters/Kolyan Indirovich|Kolyan Indirovich]], had recently died from the stress of daily assaults on their home. While the party paid their respects, [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] found herself unsure which God the household prayed to, and [[Kexik]], wary of how dead the burgomaster _actually_ was, called on his divine energy to try and sense for undeath but finding only [[Greavard]], happily following [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]].

[[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]] told them more of [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd Von Zarovich]], a vampire who ruled over Barovia and was responsible for the attacks against the house that led to his father's death - attacks which had ceased upon the burgomaster's death. Strahd has recently taken an interest in [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], and the village's close proximity to [[Castle Ravenloft]] was the driving force behind Ismark's desire to get his sister away from Strahd and his spies, who Ismark claimed "were everywhere".

Before Ireena could leave, her father had to be buried. [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]], [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], and [[Players/index|Short Supply]] transported the burgomaster's coffin on a wagon to the church in the northern end of the village. [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], spotting a raven perched on the church as they approached, drew his weapon to take a shot at it, but was stopped by [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]], who noted that Barovians found ravens to be a good omen.

Inside the church they found Father Donavich, sleep deprived and hoarse, praying amid a ruining church, with the constant screams and pleas of his son coming from the basement. His son, Doru, had led an attack against Strahd the previous year and returned as a vampire spawn. Unwilling to end his son's undeath, the priest had locked him in the basement, spending his days praying to the Morninglord for guidance on how to save his son.

The party and Ismark were able to convince Donavich to perform funerary rites for the burgomaster. Following [[Specimen - 88|88]] and [[Kexik]] digging the grave, the burgomaster was interred while a prayer to the Morninglord was recited. [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] and [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] noted the presence of a tall man in dark clothing watching from the mists at the edge of the cemetery. When Rick noted his envy of the cool fog effects, a startled [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] fled into the church. [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]] was convinced it was [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] and, with the funeral ended, wanted the party to retreat back to the relative safety of the burgomaster's mansion.

[[Kexik]] and [[Specimen - 88|88]], concerned with the vampire spawn, attempted to convince Donavich to end his son's suffering, but the priest's unwavering faith and love for his son made him unwilling to condone such an action. The party agreed to acquiesce to Donavich's wishes - for the time being at least.

With Ismark having to deal with being the newly appointed burgomaster, he invited the party to stay the night and prepare before heading to Vallaki in the morning, with Ireena fielding any more questions the party may have.

Before heading to bed, [[Specimen - 88|88]] and [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] felt compelled to eat the remainder of their dream pie. Rick did not feel the same compulsion, but that was likely due to him already snacking on the pie throughout the day.

[[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] has a dream:

> You find yourself standing at crossroads, where a raven beckons you towards the northern fork. You see a sign pointing that direction, but when you try to read it, you can't understand the words.
>
> Your surroundings blur, and you're delivered a camp next to a small lake that seems to be fed by a river. The camp contains 5 colorful, round tents, pitched outside four barrel-topped wagons. There's a larger tent on the shore of the lake. The camp is otherwise completely empty.
>
> You find yourself psychically drawn towards the interior of the large tent. Without consciously doing so, you start walking towards it. Inside, there sits a woman, her her face and form concealed by a dark purple cloak.
>
> She draws a card from a deck that sits atop the table. You can make out the card clearly - written atop the card are the words "The Mists".
>
> The woman speaks. "Claire, The distance between us renders my Sight blurred and obscured. Please, join me at Tser Pool to learn what the future holds."

Upon awaking, Claire finds that the rest of the party have had the same dream. When they ask Ireena if she is aware of the whereabouts of Tser Pool, she shares a map with the party, noting that it is on their way to [[Vallaki]].
