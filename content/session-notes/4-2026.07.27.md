# Session Overview

After taking a short rest, our adventurers continued to search for [[Walter Durst|Walter]]. After being unable the raise the portcullis, they explored a series of cells nearby, with [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] finding a secret entrance into a ritual chamber upon which rested an infant-sized bundle. Approaching the bundle, [[Kexik]] finds only a dagger, while a chorus of phantom cult members chanting that "One Must Die! One Must Die!". Unwilling to sacrifice one of their own, the displeased cultists summoned a grotesque mound of flesh from a refuse pile nearby. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]], sensing a loophole, suggested that the Flesh Mound be the one to die, and the party attacked.

After [[Specimen - 88]] dealt a critical hit, the party made a gruesome discovery - the infant [[Walter Durst|Walter]], buried in the heart of the flesh mound. With each blow, globs of flesh were hacked off the mass, exposing more and more of the infant.  [[Kexik]] attempted to wrest [[Walter Durst|Walter]] from the pulsating mass of flesh, but it was still connected to the host of flesh with sinewy tendrils weaving in and out of the small body.  While [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] and [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] attempted to raise the portcullis to escape, the rest of the party continued their assault. As the continued attacks cleaved off additional mounds of flesh, [[Specimen - 88]] was finally able to pull the baby's body free. The remaining flesh melted away, leaving the corpse of the infant -  now finally at peace.

With [[Walter Durst|Walter]] freed, the party was assailed by a series of visions of the Durst famiy's fall from grace and the corruption of [[Elizabeth Durst]] to the Dark Powers leading to the demise of the family. With the curse broken, the spell keeping the house in good repair failed, and the party was forced to flee the destruction of a house still intent on claiming them as its victims.

With each room entered, the party faced a different challenge that tested their skills:

- [[Specimen - 88]] used his athleticism to wrench open the portcullis
- [[Specimen - 88]] vaulted over a ghoul in a show of acrobatics
- [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] calmed taxidermied wolves come back to life in the den with her animal handling
- [[Kexik]] was unable to impress a increasingly bricked-over front door with his performance, reinforcing his place in the troupe as the "Combat Guy"
- [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] was able to perceive a weak point in the entryway allowing the group to escape back to the den.
- [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] used his fast hands to prevent the party from being washed away down a set of stairs by a fetid flood
- [[Kexik]] was unable to intimidate animated suits of armour
- [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] used her insight to determine the best time to jam her shield into the spinning blades keeping the party from exiting to the balcony
- [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] used his arcane knowledge to discern that the fatal drop from the balcony was an illusion

The party was able to play to their strengths to escape from the balcony to the courtyard. After seeing [[Specimen - 88]] roll his ankle leaping from the balcony, [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] took the sensible approach and shimmied down a drain pipe, impressively not catching his robes on the way down. The party sprinted across the courtyard to make it through the closing gates attempting to keep them in place, but the entire party was able to make it through before the gates slammed shut forever. With the exception of Claire, who was celebrating the vindication of a vision come to life, the party watched in horror as the house sunk into the earth leaving an eerie quiet.

Exhausted from their labours, and haunted by the things they witnessed, the troupe was unwilling to remain here to rest, so they mounted their carriage and continued down the road until coming to a village that seemed unnaturally empty. This may have worked in the party's favour however, as there was barely anyone around to take offense to the loud procession they made down  the cobbled road, the sound of their ruckus echoing through the empty street. Shortly after entering the town, the party took notice of an old woman selling pies. She introduced herself as [[Granny]], and offers each member of the party a free pie. When offered payment, she refuses, insisting that the party looks hungry and that her pies offer her customers an escape from the misery and despair of everyday Barovian life. [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] and [[Specimen - 88]], both ravenous from their adventures, immediately dug into the offered pie, while the rest of the party showed some restraint. After asking Granny where they could find accomodations for the night, they took their leave and headed to the Blood of the Vine tavern.

Upon entering the tavern, [[Kexik]] immediately approaches the bar and attempts to persuade the barkeep to allow the troupe to perform in exchange for lodging. Charismatic as ever, [[Kexik]] is unsuccessful in his attempt, but is overheard by another, who invites the party to sit with him, offering them all a glass of wine in return for their company. The man introduces himself as [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]], son of [[Kolyan Indirovich]], the burgomaster of the [[Village of Barovia]]. After hearing Kexik's introduction of [[FMPc]], he indicates that the troupe may have luck in [[Vallaki]], where festivals are a frequent occurrence.  He then pleads with the party to help them spirit his sister [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] out of the village and take her to Vallaki so she will be safe from the attention of [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Lord Strahd]]. Knowing the party has need of lodging, [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]] offers to put the group up for the night at the tavern in exchange for a favour, requesting they come to the Burgomaster's Mansion in the morning.

The party agrees, and [[Ismark Kol­yanovich|Ismark]] departs, leaving Short Notice to retire for the evening and make sense of what they have just been through, each of them finding within themselves a newfound resolve, as they forge their paths in response to their recent experiences.
