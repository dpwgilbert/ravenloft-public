# Session Overview

Having the location of  [[St. Andral's Church]] and [[St. Andral's Orphanage]] added to their map of [[Vallaki]] by the city guard, the party made their way through town. With the orphanage on the other side of town being their first stop, the party discovered some of the [[Vallaki#Discovered Districts|districts]] and [[Vallaki#Shops|shops]] of [[Vallaki]] as they travelled. While sharing the [[FMPc]] brand with the citizens by parading their cart through town was a good marketing move, the streets were somewhat too narrow to make traversing with a caravan a simple matter, and the party put up their horses and wagon at the stables near the center of town.

Arriving at  the [[St. Andral's Orphanage|orphanage]], the party was met at the door by a teenage girl with a toddler on her hip, who invited them in and excused herself to find the headmistress of the orphanage, [[Claudia Belsasco]]. [[Specimen - 88|88]], concerned with how the children would feel about his appearance, remained outside. While the children all appeared to be in good spirits, it was hard for the party not to notice that almost all of the children had some degree of bruising outside of what would be expected from childrens' normal play. When asked about the bruises, none of the children could seem to recall how or when they had appeared. When the children confided to [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]] that they believed that the orphanage was haunted, he convinced them to show him around their rooms, where he found the legs of the beds carved with runes, which [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] discovered to be Infernal. They also mentioned that one of the older children, [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]] had recently fallen ill, and was unable to be roused from his sleep.

When the headmistress appeared, she accepted the new young children into her charge, noting that three other children - Lazlo, Hans, and Franz - had recently gone missing. She had hoped that the new children were those three, and noted that she had hired wolf hunters - based on their tracking abilities - to find the missing children. When [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] and [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] offered their services to try and heal [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]], they quickly found that his illness was magical, and could not be cured with traditional remedies and spells. The headmistress gave her blessing for the party to search through the orphanage for a potential cause and cure for [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]].

[[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] tried carving the sun of the Morninglord into the bedpost, and found that the Infernal runes disappeared when this was done. The party moved upstairs and found a boarded up window, where children noted that another boy, Cedric, had recently died after falling out of the window. Finding a ladder to the attic, the party climbed up the ladder to hear scratching noises coming from a young boy sitting at the far end of the attic. When the party approached, the boy turned to show that the scratching came as the boy whittled some wood with a kitchen knife. The party continued to question the boy, who gave increasingly unsettling answers and became increasingly aggressive, until he eventually slumped down - a shadowy demon rising from the boy's body and attacking the party.

[[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] was somehow able to encase the shadow demon in ice, restricting it's movement until it was able to break free. Rick broke out his new kazoo, and what he lacked in experience he made up for with enthusiasm, damaging the demon, and the eardrums of everyone else. After hearing the commotion from down below, [[Specimen - 88|88]] charged up to the attic to join the fray. The party continued dealing out heavy blows to the demon, who was unable to return the favour. After a barrage of attacks, the demon - in a desparate bid to recover - risked attacks of opportunity to target the squishy wizard. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]], boosted by his teammates, was able to narrowly avoid the assault, following which both [[Specimen - 88|88]] and [[Kexik]] rained critical strikes down on the demon as retribution, with Kexik's divine strike felling the creature.

As the shadows faded, the young boy sat there sobbing. He introduced himself as [[Characters/Felix|Felix]], and explained how he found a locket next to Cedric's body, and that's when the bruising of the children and [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]]'s illness began. After soothing the boy, the party brought him down to the living room to reunite him with his fellow orphans. Checking on [[Characters/Milivoj|Milivoj]], they found him awake and responsive, the fever disappearing with the demon. The [[Claudia Belsasco|headmistress]], in appreciation for solving the mysteries of the orphanage, provided the party with two _potions of healing_ and invited them to the orphanage whenever they needed a place to rest. Not wanting the item that appeared to be responsible for the troubles to continue, they requested the locket, which [[Characters/Felix|Felix]] had surrendered to the headmistress at her request. She agreed, and fetched the locket from a locked desk drawer in her office. The locket appeared to be made of tin, and was inscribed with something [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] recognized as Celestial, though he could not read it. While [[Kexik]] wished to destroy the locket, [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] held onto it, hoping to decipher the inscription at a later date.

With the new orphans safely secured in an orphanage with one less demon than when they arrived, the party crossed the street and approached the steps of [[St. Andral's Church]].
