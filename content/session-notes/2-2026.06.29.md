# Session Overview

Our party found themselves on the third floor of the Durst House, having recently defeated an animated set of armour. [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]]'s keen eye noted that he could likely salvage some of that armour for use by our poorly equipped adventurers.

[[Kexik]], exploring a nearby closet, found himself beset upon by an aggressive (and well made) animated broom. Being closest, Kexik was the target of the broom's ire and took damage from multiple attacks, fortunately buffed by [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] with a well-intentioned ~~caress~~ shove. The slight wizard seems to be able to do the most damage to the broom, but Kexik, not wanting to be shown up by a stick and some straw, was able to land a blow and strike down the broom. In a burst of inspiration, Jerry took some soap and sheets from the closet and fashioned himself a makeshift flail, impressing all with his ingenuity.

Moving into the master suite, the group found, in addition to some jewelry,  a letter tucked into a jewelry box from [[Elizabeth Durst|Mrs. Elizabeth Durst]] to a Mrs. Petrovna outlining how sacrificing an "innocent" to a planned ceremony may wield better results. [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]] expressed fear that [[Walter Durst|Walter]] may be the innocent mentioned.

The party ran into the ghost of the children's nursemaid, [[Margaret]], who was cowering in a corner, With Zavran's assurances that the party meant no harm, she directed them to the adjoining nursery, but Walter's crib was empty. Confused, Margaret said he must be with [[Rose Durst|Rose]] and [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]] and directed them to a set of stairs hidden behind a mirror leading up to the attic - a stairway that had been previously discovered by the party after [[Specimen - 88]] broke his way through a heavily barred door opposite the mirror.

Advancing to the attic, the party used the key they had found in a secret room on the second floor and made a grim discovery - the skeletons of [[Rose Durst|Rose]] and [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]]. Upon discovery, Rose and Thorn's ghosts appeared - not corporeal as they were outside - and now seemingly aware of their deaths. They explained that they would be locked in their room while their parents fought the monster, but this time no one ever came to unlock the door and they eventually starved to death. Rose still was concerned for [[Walter Durst|Walter]], and asked the party to continue to look for him, pointing out a hidden stairway on a replica dollhouse found in the room.

Rose, noting the party's lack of offensive capabilities, noted that her father had a study on the first floor that contained weapons that might be of aid against the monster. When asked by Kexik if anything could be done to put them to rest, Rose indicated that there was a family crypt in the basement where their bodies could be interred. Ever the noble paladin, Kexik wrapped their bodies in a bedsheet and carried them with the intent to do just that.

Carefully exploring the remainder of the attic, the party found the staircase that Rose had indicated and Jerry found a chest containing the body of  [[Margaret]], the nursemaid.

Cue a montage of the party moving through the house looking to better equip themselves before proceeding down the secret staircase to the basement. Jerry managed to scavenge three sets of armour from the various suits the party had found throughout the house and within the study, a selection of crossbows was stored in a seemingly impenetrable cabinet that resisted both Specimen 88's attempts to force it open and subsequent lockpicking attempts by Jerry and Rick. Fortunately the weapons were secured after Zavran used the key they had found previously.

Descending into the basement, the party could hear the frightened cries of a baby that, with keen perception, could be identified as coming from the ~~south~~ west. The party found a pair of crypts, likely the ones that Rose had mentioned. Both were open - one was unmarked, and the other had [[Walter Durst]] etched into a slab meant to seal the crypt, but the crypt was empty.

Curiosity pulled the party away from the crypts to a pair of living quarters, the first filled only with moldy straw pallets, the second containing rooms in better condition, each with a corresponding footlocker which the party, in a combination of brute strength and finesse, were able to access. The chests contained a series of treasures, along with a disturbing journal. Bound in grimy black leather, this journal maintained a list of names, physical descriptions, and the negative results of some event. The second column logs the physical description of each victim named in the first column, while the third column bears a single word for each entry: "Unsuccessful."

Kexik investigated a well at the centre of the second set of quarters, in the hopes of blessing some of the well water for use later in their adventures, but that dream was quickly squashed as Claire brusquely indicated she had no such ability, and Kexik found the water too fetid to convert on his own.

The night closed as Claire, whether through providence or blind luck, opened the final chest in the quarters revealing a silvered mace to replace the one stolen in Daggerford. With a gleam in her eye and a smile on her face, she drops the makeshift flail and club fashioned earlier and raises the mace in triumph.
