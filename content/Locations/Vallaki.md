A bustling town led by [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]].

Residents appear quite happy, with wide smiles everywhere - though the sincerity of the smiles is in question.

## Discovered Districts

### Labour District

The vast majority of labourers and workers spend their days in and around the stockyard, where they perform trade tasks like producing firewood from lumber and cooping barrels. The area has a very small smithy, which produces nails, horseshoes, and hinges instead of arms or armour. The district is also home to the coffin shop, as well as some of Vallaki's livestock.

### The Town Square

The central district consists of the town square, the [[Blue Water Inn]], the stables, and the majority of Vallaki's shops.

### Residents District

While Vallakians technically have homes all around the town, most settle into the western quarter. The area is crowded and the land beyond the houses, closest to the walls, is made up of livestock pens and minor farmland. While larger farms exist beyond the walls, Vallakians prefer to work within the safety of their gates and vie for jobs at those fields. In addition, the bulk of these residential Vallakians flock to [[St. Andral's Church]] near the western gate, which is one of the largest buildings in town.

## Shops

#### The Dusklight Supply Shop

This general store is located in the town square, a few minutes walk from the [[Blue Water Inn]]. It sells a menagerie of basic crafting gear, including rope, candles, torches, lanterns, crowbars and most standard adventuring equipment.

#### Thimdul's Armaments

Thimdul's Armaments is a smithy located in the guard district.

#### Third Eye Potions and Sundries

The local apothecary lives in a tiny shop in the north of the labor district.

#### Yonvich & Sons

The bookshop is located in the town square.

#### Selkovsky Sewing

In addition to a collection of smaller workshops in the labor district, their main shop is located in the town square.
