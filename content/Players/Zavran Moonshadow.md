## A Path Chosen

The soot-stained pages of Zavran’s spellbook sat open on the tavern table, ruined.   While the rest of the party nursed their wounds, the wizard stared into the dark corner of the room, his jaw set, his mind racing through the dark, brutal realities of the Death House.

He had watched his companions bleed. [[Kexik]] fought for honor, [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] for faith, [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]] for creation, [[Specimen - 88|88]] for rage, and [[Fabulous Rick Landry|"Fabulous" Rick]] for gold. But as Zavran looked into the dark streets of Barovia, he knew none of those things would truly protect the innocent. Honor was blind. Faith was slow. Gold was corruptible.

What the world needed—what this nightmare realm required—was **Justice**.

Not the squishy, lenient kind, but a cold, absolute, vigilant force operating from the shadows. A Symbol that struck fear into the hearts of superstitious, cowardly cultists.\
Yet, in the narrow halls of that house, his traditional wizardry had failed him. A heavy tome was too slow to draw. verbal components took too long to speak. And worse, he had nearly starved down in that dungeon; all the grand arcana in the world meant nothing when your body lacked the dense, starchy foundation required for absolute endurance.

He looked down at the table. Next to his burnt book sat two things: a discarded scrap of parchment and a single, unpeeled potato Rick had snitched from the kitchen.\
Zavran’s dark eyes narrowed. _The potato,_ he thought, staring at its humble, rugged skin. _It is born in the mud, forged in the absolute dark, unyielding, cheap, and nourishing. It is the silent, underground backbone of life._

And then he looked at the parchment.

His ash-stained fingers moved with precise, silent brutality. Fold. Crease. Tuck.  He didn't just fold the paper—he broke its form, bending it to his unyielding spirit. With three swift, angular creases, the flat sheet transformed in his palm into a geometric, razor-sharp silhouette. A bat.

He breathed a whisper of arcane force into its hollow core. The paper creature twitched, snapped its angular wings, and hovered in the dim tavern light—a silent sentinel carved from pure geometry.

_A heavy spellbook is a liability,_ Zavran realized, his voice dropping an octave as he stared through the shadows. _It can be taken. But paper is everywhere. Light. Lethal. Silent._\
A sheet of paper could be folded into a sharp, throw-able batarang of pure force. A humble potato could sustain the dark vigil when all other supplies failed. Combined with the geometry of the origami arts, he could strike from the shadows, vanish into the night, and inflict crisp, perfectly folded punishment upon the wicked.

The paper bat circled the dark corners of the ceiling before descending, landing silently on the potato on the table.

Zavran pulled his cowl low over his face, shading his eyes in deep, dramatic shadow. He closed his ruined tome for the last time and reached for a fresh, crisp stack of parchment.

"The night is young," Zavran muttered, his voice a gravelly whisper to the dark room. "And this city... needs a hero."
