# Description

Kexik's birth and origin is somewhat of a mystery. Raised in a monastery in the city of Calimport, Kexik knows only that he was found in swaddling clothes, at the main door to the temple he was raised in.

Raised in the great city of Calimport, from an early age Kexik was fascinated by stories of the Djinn. Studying these mystic creatures went beyond a simple passion. Legends of powerful Djinn performing miraculous feats of wonder, along with his innate sense of justice and the desire to protect those who could not protect themselves, lead Kexik to learn the art of martial combat. Combining his training with his faith, Kexik found himself in the unique position of becoming a guardian not just within the monastery, but in the streets surrounding the monastery as well.

Over time. as his reputation grew, more and more of the noble houses of Calimport approached Kexik to pledge allegiance to their cause. It would be a great thing to have such a noble warrior of Dragonborn descent among the honor guard of any of the great houses. But Kexik refused, sighting his devotion to his temple and his faith for not bending the knee to any one of the great houses of Calimport. This lead to suspicion within these noble houses, and before long, Kexik found himself the target of assassins. One night, Kexik woke to find dozens of large spiders crawling over his body and descending on strands of glimmering silk from the ceiling in his cell. In a panic, Kexik launched himself from his bed and began squashing the skittering monsters. His naturally tough hide prevented any of the creatures from sinking their fangs into him and poisoning him, but the event that night secured in his mind that Kexik would find no more rest while in Calimport.

To keep the monastery and it's inhabitants safe, Kexik decided to embark on a holy quest to learn more about his ancestry, and to teach others about the legendary Djinn of his homeland. Bidding farewell to the only home he had ever known, Kexik booked passage on a ship headed north. After several weeks, with his purse slowly running dry, Kexik made landfall in the town of Daggerford, where he now finds himself wondering if this journey was a mistake, or if he will ever be able to learn about his past while teaching others about the Djinn of his homeland.

### Phobia

Arachnophobia (Spiders)

## A Path Chosen

Despite being bone weary, Kexik sleeps fitfully. He is wracked by terrifying images of the horrendous, lumbering mound of flesh that surrounded the tiny, mutilated form of [[Walter Durst|Walter]], the infant he was unable to save. He reaches out to the baby but is unable to grasp it before it simply turns to a grizzly slurry and runs through his clawed hands. His vision is filled with myriad nightmare creatures that he and his companions cannot escape. The weight of his failures weighs heavily upon him. The images shift and he is no longer in the house of horrors but suddenly finds himself lost, alone and once again surrounded by mist. He calls out and hears only the faint echo of his own voice. Looking down at his hands, his claws are shriveled and weak. He looks up and he sees [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], lying still and pale, his form twisted and broken as though having fallen from a great height. The large gnome eyes suddenly snap open, white and lifeless. "Why Kexik? Why did you leave me to fall?" The vision shifts, and suddenly 88 is kneeling on the ground, whimpering as a child. [[Specimen - 88|88]] looks to Kexik, terror on his face. "The walls are closing in. Help me Kexik!!" And suddenly 88 starts to scream a blood curdling high pitched wail. The cry is replaced by the snapping of bone and the tearing of muscle and sinew as 88’s form is crushed to a pulpy mass. Kexik blinks the vision away and he sees [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]], covered in filth brushing violently at his face and hands. Rick looks to Kexik with a confused expression. “I can’t get it off!! Why won’t it come off!!??!!” Kexik watches, his bile rising as Rick begins to scrub the flesh from his face. The muscle is soon revealed, blood oozing out through his hollowed cheeks and yet still he scrubs. Through the flesh and sinew stark white bone begins to appear. Tears stream down Kexik’s face and the shame of his failures burns. [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] is there suddenly. Cringing and looking to the sky. She is sobbing, her hands clasped over her ears. “Make it stop!! It’s too loud!! Make it stop!!” Kexik reaches out to her as her form is suddenly engulfed in a bolt of lightning so intense that Kexik’s scales are scorched from the heat, his nostrils are filled with the scent of burnt hair and flesh. Stumbling through the mist, trying desperately to escape, Kexik suddenly bumps into the shadowed form of [[Zavran Moonshadow|Zavran]]. “I told you never to touch me!! DON’T TOUCH ME!!” Zavran screams at Kexik, the sound is ear shattering. Zavran’s mouth and jaw distends and continues to stretch as the scream continues beyond reason. The cheeks begin to tear with a wet ripping sound until Zavran’s jaw simply hangs loose and open, the tongue lolling from side to side and yet the scream does not end. 

Kexik turns and runs through the mist. He runs until his lungs are bursting and his legs feel like jelly. Tears of shame spill down his face, and yet still he runs. After what feels like an eternity, through the mists, a voice calls out to him. Kexik slows his pace, gasping for air and tries to hear the voice over the sound of the blood pounding in his ears. Again the voice calls out to him, closer this time, gentle, though filled with a deep rumble. A voice he feels he should recognize, but cannot place. “Stop my child”, the voice says. “Remember who you are and where you come from.” Kexik falls to his knees and closes his eyes. Taking a deep breath, shuddering breath, and then another, and another, Kexik feels his heart begin to slow. Kexik gathers himself before warily reopening his eyes. Standing before him is the shadowy form of his old master from the orphanage in Calimport. The image appears to slowly be shifting, especially around the eyes as Kexik struggles to focus on any one feature. “Rest my child. You have been forced to endure much in a short time, but destiny is not done with you. You do not know it yet, but a great quest is about to be set upon you. And while you may not believe it, you already possess the strength to see it through.” The shadowy form smiles down at Kexik and places a firm but gentle hand upon his shoulder. Warmth radiates from the touch, washing away much of the lingering fear and terror that held Kexic in its grip. “You must armour your heart and surround yourself in the knowledge and faith that you can bring justice to this cursed land. You must face these challenges with conviction and never lose sight in your mission to defend the weak, and to release back into death that which walks in undeath. Your ancestors are watching over you. Draw on their strength in times of darkness and they will light your path” The shadowy form smiles down at Kexik, and just before the vision fades, the eyes shift ever so slightly. For the briefest moment, the eyes are serpentine and flash with a hint of bronze.

In his dream, Kexik breathes deeply. A sense of righteous fury begins to roil in his soul. The horrific flashes of his companions slowly burn away and are replaced with a renewed sense of divine purpose. He reaches out with his senses and feels the presence of his ancestors. Watching, waiting. 

Slowly, Kexik opens his eyes. He is no longer asleep, but lays in his bed, breathing slowly and steadily. The sheets lay on the floor, tossed aside during his horrific nightmare. Glancing around, Kexik can see a single ray of moonlight shining through the window of his room. Rising slowly, he crosses the room to stand in the pale moonlight. Grasping the amulet around his neck, Kexik closes his eyes and begins to whisper a silent prayer to his ancestors. The heavy sense of dread he felt in his nightmare slowly slips away, leaving him with a renewed sense of purpose. Kexik swears an Oath to his ancestors and on his soul that he will defend those who cannot defend themselves. He will wreak havoc on those who would pray upon the innocent. But most importantly, he will stop at nothing to lay low the undead scourge of this realm. 

Thus Kexik has taken his [Oath of Zeal](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/ghpg/chapter-2-classes-subclasses#OathofZeal).
