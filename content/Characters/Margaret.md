Former nursemaid of [[Rose Durst|Rose]], [[Thorn Durst|Thorn]], and [[Walter Durst|Walter]] who haunts her quarters as a specter. Withdrawn and shy, Margaret does not fully understand how or why she died. She is confused, and frequently jumps between awareness and ignorance of her own state of undeath, sometimes in the same sentence.

The party found her body stuffed into a trunk in an attic storage room.
