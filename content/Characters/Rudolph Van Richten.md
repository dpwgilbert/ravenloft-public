A man famous for his writings on monsters, who clashed with Strahd many years ago, disappearing thereafter. How we came to Barovia, and whether or not he was able to escape its mists, is unknown.

The party found a page from his _Guide to Vampires_ in their room in the Burgomaster's Mansion.
