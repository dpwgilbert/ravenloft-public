An elderly woman who came to [[Claire Ich Al-ErRoar|Claire]] and the rest of [[Players/index|Short Supply]] in their dreams, beaconing them to visit her at the [[Tser Pool Encampment]].

She is the elder for a group of [[Groups/Vistani|Vistani]] who make the encampment their home.

When the party arrived, she informed them of their fates - a group brought together to overthrow [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]] and free Barovia from the mists - and consulted her Tarokka card to give the party the advantages they would need to be successful.
