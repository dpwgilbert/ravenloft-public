Friendly old woman selling "dream pies" in the streets of Barovia.

Revealed to be a hag who, along with her children, were using the children of Barovia to create the Dream Pies that gave people - including [[Fabulous Rick Landry|Rick]], [[Jerry Rig|Jerry]], and [[Specimen - 88|88]] - a deep sleep filled with wonderful dreams.
