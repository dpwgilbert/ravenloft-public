Son of [[Characters/Kolyan Indirovich|Kolyan Indirovich]], the burgomaster of the Village of Barovia, and brother to [[Characters/Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]].

The party meets Ismark in the Blood of the Vine tavern, where he requests the party's help, putting them up in the tavern for the night as incentive.

Ismark recruits the party to escort his sister to the town of [[Vallaki]], hoping to keep her away from [[Characters/Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]].

With the passing of his father, Ismark becomes the new burgomaster after his father's funeral.
