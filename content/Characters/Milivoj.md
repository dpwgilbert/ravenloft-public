Teenage boy, found sick at the [[St. Andral's Orphanage]] in [[Vallaki]], having fallen into a feverish coma.

Upon the defeat of [[Characters/Felix|Felix]]'s demon, Milivoj awoke, free of illness.

Discovered and stole the bones of St. Andral for a hooded figure with a dark beard in return for 300 GP, which he used to support the church and pay for the wolf hunters to go searching for three fellow orphans who had recently run away.
