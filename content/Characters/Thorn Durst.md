A young boy, protected by his sister [[Rose Durst|Rose]], who fled his house when the monsters his parents were fighting in the basement were too loud. They requested the party's help in rescuing their baby brother Walter from the house while their parents battled a monster in the basement.

Thorn's body was found locked in his room in the attic, having died from starvation.

After Thorn's body was laid to rest in his crypt, he, along with his sister Rose, faded from existence.
